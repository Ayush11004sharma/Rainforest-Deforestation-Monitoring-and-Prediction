Conclusions¶

Model Performance¶

  • Historical Baseline achieves best test MAE (26,092 ha) — deforestation is persistent

  • Random Forest captures nonlinear patterns (R²=0.835)

  • All models evaluated chronologically — no data leakage

Risk Assessment¶

  • Very High risk: Maranhão, Rondônia, Pará, Mato Grosso, Bahia

  • These regions show high recent loss rates and increasing trends

Important Caveats¶

  • Forecasts are estimates based on historical patterns only

  • Cannot predict policy changes, fires, or economic shifts

  • Risk categories are relative rankings, not absolute standards

  • Feature importance ≠ causation
